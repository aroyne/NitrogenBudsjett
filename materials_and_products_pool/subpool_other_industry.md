### Flows that are zero or neglected:

* **MP.OP-EF.TR-Ammonia as fuel-NH3** is set to zero because there is negligible use of ammonia as fuel today.

### Flows added or changed compared with Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025-->:

* **MP.OP-AG.MM-Ammonia for straw treatment-NH3** is not in the guidelines. Ammonia is used to treat straw for feed in Norway; about half of it is emitted (NFR 3I) and the rest is eaten by the animals.
* **MP.OP-FS.FO-Mineral fertilizer-Nmix** is not in the flow tables of the guidelines, but Schäppi et al. (2025) recommend adding a flow from MP.OP or AG.MM to FS.FO in countries where forest fertilization occurs in relevant quantities. The same flow is included in the Swedish NNB (Moldan et al., 2025)<!--cite:moldan_where_2025-->.
* **RW.RW-MP.OP-Ammonia import-Nmix** is not in the guidelines, where imported ammonia would be part of **RW.RW-MP.OP-Other goods import-Nmix**. We keep it as a separate flow because ammonia is a raw material for the fertilizer industry and is used in the calculation of **AT.AT-MP.OP-Ammonia synthesis N2 fixation-N2**.
