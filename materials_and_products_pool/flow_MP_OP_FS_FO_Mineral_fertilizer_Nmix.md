# Mineral Fertilizer for Forestry

<iframe src="../output_files/plots/MP_OP_FS_FO_Mineral_fertilizer_Nmix.html" width="100%" height="400px" frameborder="0" scrolling="no"></iframe>

### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
**MP.OP-FS.FO-Mineral fertilizer-Nmix** is nitrogen for forest fertilization. This flow is not part of the guidelines but has been added because it is a significant flow in Norway, as was also done in the Swedish NNB (Moldan et al., 2025)<!--cite:moldan_where_2025-->. We have used data from SSB on area of forest fertilized and assumed a standard value of 15 kgN/da (Dalen, 2017)<!--cite:dalen_lonnsomt_2017-->. Fertilized area before 1997 is taken from Figure 2 in Landbruksdirektoratet (2021)<!--cite:landbruksdirektoratet_vurdering_2021-->.

**How the numbers are derived**

- From 1997, SSB's forest culture statistics ([Skogkultur](https://www.ssb.no/skogkultur), [table 05543](https://www.ssb.no/statbank/table/05543)) give the fertilized forest area (daa) per year.
- The statistics are based on Landbruksdirektoratet's forest fund (skogfond) database and only include measures financed through the forest fund or with state subsidies; fertilization paid for in other ways is not included.
- For 1985–1996 the area is read from the figure of fertilized forest area in Landbruksdirektoratet (2021)<!--cite:landbruksdirektoratet_vurdering_2021-->.
- The area is multiplied by 15 kg N/daa, which is the usual practice in Norway: a single dose of about 150 kg N/ha about 10 years before harvest (Landbruksdirektoratet, 2021)<!--cite:landbruksdirektoratet_vurdering_2021-->.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is 0.4–1.0 kt N per year in the late 1980s and early 1990s and below 0.2 kt N in 2003–2015.
- A state subsidy of up to 40% for forest fertilization as a climate measure was introduced in 2016, and subsidies were paid for 310,000 daa in 2016–2020 (Landbruksdirektoratet, 2021)<!--cite:landbruksdirektoratet_vurdering_2021-->; the flow rose to about 1.3 kt N in 2016–2017 (84,000–91,000 daa).
- In 2022 only about 3,100 daa were fertilized (0.05 kt N).
