# Industrial Emissions (N2O)

### Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
#### Flow description

- **MP.OP-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O** is N<sub>2</sub>O from industrial processes, as advised by Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025-->: the sum of 2.B Chemical industry, 2.C Metal industry, 2.G Other product manufacture and use and 2.H Other (2.A Mineral industry and 2.D Non-energy products from fuels report no N<sub>2</sub>O). The main source is nitric acid production.

#### Data sources

- N<sub>2</sub>O from industrial processes in the UNFCCC Common Reporting Tables (CRT), Table 2(I), of the national greenhouse gas inventory. N<sub>2</sub>O from nitric acid production is reported by the plants; the methods are documented in the National Inventory Document (Miljødirektoratet, 2026)<!--cite:miljodirektoratet_nid_2026-->.

#### Interpretations

- The chemical industry (2.B, nitric acid production) accounts for over 90% of the flow. Emissions are substantial, at least before 2009.
- Emissions were about 4.4 kt N/year<!--val:MP.OP-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O:1990--> in 1990, fell from 4.3 kt N<!--val:MP.OP-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O:2005--> in 2005 to about 1.1 kt N<!--val:MP.OP-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O:2010--> in 2010 after N<sub>2</sub>O-reducing catalysts were installed in the nitric acid plants, and have been 0.5–1.0 kt N<!--range:MP.OP-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O:2011-2024--> since.
- Category 2.G (mainly N<sub>2</sub>O used as anaesthetic and propellant) contributes about 0.06 kt N/year.
