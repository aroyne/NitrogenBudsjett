### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
**MP.OP-AG.SM-Mineral fertilizer-Nmix** is domestically produced mineral fertilizer used in agriculture, found as (total domestic use) – (import), where both use and import are given in FAOSTAT Fertilizer by nutrient (FAO, 2025)<!--cite:fao_fertilizer_2025-->. FAOSTAT Fertilizer by nutrient has not yet published a 2024 figure at the time of writing; the 2024 value is a flat carry-forward of 2023, with additional uncertainty (±50%) applied to reflect that it is not a real, independently observed value.
