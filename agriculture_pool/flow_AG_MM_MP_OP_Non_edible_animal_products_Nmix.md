### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025--> advises using FAOSTAT Commodity Balances (non-food). For Norway this statistic only contains wool for 4 individual years and we therefore use data for wool from Landbruksdirektoratet (Landbruksdirektoratet, 2025)<!--cite:landbruksdirektoratet_leveransedata-slakt-2005-2012_2025--> for 2005-2024; for earlier years, we use the number of sheep (SSB table 03710) and extrapolate from a linear regression found between sheep and wool for 2005-2024. In addition, we use numbers for raw hides and skins from FAOSTAT Crops and livestock products. N contents are taken from Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025-->.

**How the numbers are derived.** Wool is the amount of wool delivered by farmers from 2005, compiled from Landbruksdirektoratet's delivery data; before 2005 it is estimated from the number of winter-fed sheep (SSB table 03710) and the relationship between sheep numbers and wool delivered in 2005–2024. Raw hides and skins are FAOSTAT production quantities. N contents are from Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025-->.

**Interpretation.** The flow is about 1.2–1.3 kt N per year until 2015 and falls to about 1.0 kt N in 2024.
