### Flow Description
Crop products for industrial use is taken from EUROSTAT Gross nutrient balance as advised by Schäppi et al. (2025). For years with missing data, we have filled in the average of all other years. Eurostat GNB has not yet published a 2024 figure at the time of writing; the 2024 value is a 3-year average of 2021-2023 (this flow is small and volatile with no clear trend), with additional uncertainty (±50%) applied to reflect that it is not a real, independently observed value.
