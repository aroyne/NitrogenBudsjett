### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
This flow combines shellfish/crustacean catch (2.8% N content, according to Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025-->, p254, UNECE Guidance Annex 6 Table 12) and wild-harvested macroalgae/seaweed, both from Fiskeridirektoratet (2025)<!--cite:fiskeridirektoratet_fangst_2025-->. The macroalgae harvest is almost all kelp (Laminaria hyperborea) and is reported as wet weight. We use 0.2 % N in wet weight (range 0.1–0.4 %), from 1–2 % N in dry matter (Sjøtun et al., 1996; Schiener et al., 2015) and 11–16 % dry matter. The 4 % N given for aquatic plants in Table 21 of Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025--> would correspond to about 25 % protein and must refer to dry weight. For 1990-1999 both components are taken from a historical compilation, and from 2000 from the species statistics.

**How the numbers are derived**

- Catch of crustaceans and molluscs from Fiskeridirektoratet multiplied by 2.8% N, plus the harvest of macroalgae (mainly kelp, wet weight) multiplied by 0.2% N (range 0.1–0.4%).
- 2000 onward from the catch statistics by species; 1990–1999 from a historical compilation.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is about 2–2.5 kt N per year up to 2005 and rises to 11 kt N in 2024, almost entirely from Antarctic krill (no catch in 2000 and 2005, 320 kt in 2024, about 9 kt N), which is caught in the Southern Ocean; snow crab and king crab contribute much less.
- Kelp contributes about 0.3–0.4 kt N per year.
