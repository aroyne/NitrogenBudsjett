### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Taken from SSB table 05280 *Totale utslipp av fosfor og nitrogen fra avløpssektoren*. Data back to 1997 are found in the series SSB Naturressurser og miljø. Due to lack of available data we set the values in 1990-1996 to be equal to that in 1997. 

**How the numbers are derived**

- SSB's statistics on discharges from the wastewater sector ([table 05280](https://www.ssb.no/statbank/table/05280)) give the total nitrogen discharged from the municipal wastewater sector, based on the municipalities' reporting through KOSTRA.
- 1997–2001 are taken from SSB's Naturressurser og miljø, and 1990–1996 are held at the 1997 value.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is about 16–20 kt N per year, falling from about 18.5 kt N in 1990–1997 to 16 kt N in 2002–2005 and rising again to about 20 kt N in 2024 with population growth.
