### Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
**PR.WW-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O** are taken from UNFCCC Common reporting tables, Table 5.

**How the numbers are derived**

- The flow is the N2O from "5.D. Wastewater treatment and discharge" in CRT Table 5 of the national greenhouse gas inventory ([Miljødirektoratet, NID 2026](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/publikasjoner/2026/mars-2026/greenhouse-gas-emissions-1990-2024-national-inventory-document/)), calculated from the N in wastewater and IPCC emission factors for treatment plants and for the effluent discharged.
- N2O is converted to N with the factor 28/44.

**Interpretation**

- The flow rises slowly from 0.09 to 0.14 kt N per year, following the population connected to wastewater treatment.
