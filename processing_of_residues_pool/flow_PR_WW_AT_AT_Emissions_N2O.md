### Description

#### Flow description

- **PR.WW-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O** is N<sub>2</sub>O from wastewater treatment and discharge.

#### Data sources

- N<sub>2</sub>O from "5.D. Wastewater treatment and discharge" in the UNFCCC Common Reporting Tables (CRT), Table 5, of the national greenhouse gas inventory ([Miljødirektoratet, NID 2026](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/publikasjoner/2026/mars-2026/greenhouse-gas-emissions-1990-2024-national-inventory-document/)), calculated from the N in wastewater and IPCC emission factors for treatment plants and for the effluent discharged.

#### Interpretations and comparisons

- The flow rises slowly from 0.09 to 0.14 kt N per year, following the population connected to wastewater treatment.
