### Description

#### Flow description

- **PR.WW-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O** is N2O from wastewater treatment and discharge.

#### Data sources

- N2O from "5.D. Wastewater treatment and discharge" in the UNFCCC Common Reporting Tables (CRT), Table 5, of the national greenhouse gas inventory ([Miljødirektoratet, NID 2026](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/publikasjoner/2026/mars-2026/greenhouse-gas-emissions-1990-2024-national-inventory-document/)), calculated from the N in wastewater and IPCC emission factors for treatment plants and for the effluent discharged.

#### Interpretations and comparisons

- The flow rises slowly from 0.09 to 0.14 kt N per year, following the population connected to wastewater treatment.

### Uncertainty

Sources with at least 2% of the variance of this flow in one of the periods, from a first-order sensitivity analysis around the central run: each source is raised by 10 % on its own, and the change in the flow is scaled with the source's uncertainty in the Monte Carlo. Shares are averages over the years of the period. They are indicative where the flow is a residual close to zero or a source has a strongly skewed distribution. Computed 2026-10-20.
