# Fuel Wood for Households

### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Taken from SSB table 09702 'Energibalansen. Vedforbruk i boliger og fritidsboliger 1990 – 2024' and an N content of 1.4 kg/t for stem wood with bark.

**How the numbers are derived**

- SSB's energy balance gives the wood burned in dwellings and holiday homes ([table 09702](https://www.ssb.no/statbank/table/09702)).
- From 2005 the figures are based on SSB's survey of households' wood consumption; earlier years build on SSB's energy statistics and household surveys.
- For 2005 the holiday-home figure is the 2006 value.
- Fuel wood is mainly stem wood with bark, much of it birch, so the N content of broadleaf stems is used: 1.4 g/kg (Table 45 in Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025-->), with a range from 0.7 g/kg (stems minus one standard deviation) to 4.3 g/kg (whole broadleaf trees including branches and foliage).
- The stem value is close to the 1.1 g/kg for stem with bark of conifers used by Hellsten et al. (2008), cited in the Swedish nitrogen budget (Jutterström et al., 2020)<!--cite:jutterstrom_swedish_2020-->; the Swedish budget uses the whole-tree value for fuel wood, as the guidance suggests when no other data are available.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is about 1.7–2.8 kt N per year.
- It rises from about 2.0 kt N in 1990 to a peak of 2.8 kt N in 2010, a cold winter, falls to about 1.9 kt N in 2015–2020 and rises again to 2.3 kt N in 2022, when electricity prices were high.
