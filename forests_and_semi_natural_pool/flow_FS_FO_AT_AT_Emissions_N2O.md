### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Taken from UNFCCC Common reporting tables, Table 4.

**How the numbers are derived**

- The flow is the N2O from "4.A. Forest land" in CRT Table 4 of the national greenhouse gas inventory ([Miljødirektoratet, NID 2026](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/publikasjoner/2026/mars-2026/greenhouse-gas-emissions-1990-2024-national-inventory-document/)), calculated with IPCC methods.
- In 2024 nearly all of it (0.57 of 0.59 kt N2O) comes from drained organic forest soils (Table 4(II)); fertilization of forest contributes 0.014 kt, mineralisation after land-use change 0.002 kt and forest fires 0.001 kt N2O.
- N2O is converted to N with the factor 28/44.
- The inventory gives an uncertainty of 201% (95% interval), modelled as a lognormal distribution with the reported value as median.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is small and nearly constant, about 0.37–0.38 kt N per year, since the area of drained organic forest soil changes little.
