# Forest Emissions (N2)

### Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Calculated based on N2O emissions from UNFCCC Common reporting tables, Table 4, and a mean N2:N2O ratio of 19.5 given by Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025--> from Butterbach-Bahl et al. (2013).

**How the numbers are derived**

- N2 from forest soils is not measured or reported.
- The flow is the N2O from forest land in CRT Table 4 (see FS.FO-AT.AT-Emissions-N2O) multiplied by an N2:N2O ratio of 19.5 ± 26.8 (range 0–46.3), calculated by Butterbach-Bahl et al. (2013) from six studies of temperate beech and spruce forests in Germany (Schäppi et al. (2025)<!--cite:schappi_annexes_2025-->, p. 157).
- Schäppi et al. (2025) also give an alternative: an average N2 loss of 1.1 kg N per ha per year from Andreae et al. (2016), used by Sweden and Germany. With about 12 million ha of forest this would give about 13 kt N per year.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is about 7.1–7.7 kt N per year and follows the N2O flow.
- Since both the N2O emissions and the ratio are very uncertain, the 95% interval is wide.
