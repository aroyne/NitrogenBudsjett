### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Calculated from the number of livestock on utmark grazing and the feed taken up per animal, calibrated against the estimate of total feed uptake on utmark in 1996 in Table 1.2 of Hegrenes & Asheim (2006)<!--cite:hegrenes_verdi_2006-->, with 150 g protein per FEm and the standard Jones factor.

**How the numbers are derived**

- SSB gives the number of livestock on utmark grazing ([table 12660](https://www.ssb.no/statbank/table/12660)), based on the applications for the production subsidy for animals on utmark: animals with at least 8 weeks on utmark up to 2008 and at least 5 weeks from 2009.
- The table covers all animals on utmark, not only those in grazing associations, and is available from 1995; sheep include lambs, and goats and horses are reported together.
- Hegrenes & Asheim (2006, Table 1.2, after Garmo & Skurdal, 1998) estimate that livestock took up 303 mill. FEm on utmark in 1996: 70% by sheep, 27% by cattle, 2% by goats and 1% by horses.
- Dividing each group's share by the number of animals in 1996 gives about 95 FEm per sheep or lamb, 336 FEm per head of cattle and 105 FEm per goat or horse per grazing season; these are applied to all years.
- The FEm are converted to N with 150 g protein per FEm (as for innmark grazing in AG.SM-AG.MM-Fodder crops-Nmix) and 1/6.25.
- For 1990–1994, sheep are extrapolated back from 1995 with the change in the number of winter-fed sheep ([SSB table 03710](https://www.ssb.no/statbank/table/03710)), and cattle, goats and horses are held at the 1995 level. The statistics on organised grazing (Organisert beitebruk) are not used, since they grew in the early 1990s as more farmers joined grazing associations and so do not show the number of animals on utmark.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is about 6.9–7.4 kt N in 1990–1996 and falls slowly to about 6.4 kt N in 2024, following the number of sheep on utmark (2.2 million in 1996, 1.8 million in 2024); the number of cattle on utmark has increased since 2017.
- The values for 1990–1994 (6.9–7.4 kt N) follow the number of sheep and are less certain than later years.
