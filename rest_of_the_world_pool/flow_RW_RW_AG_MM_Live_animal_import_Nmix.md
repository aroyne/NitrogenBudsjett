### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Is taken from FAOSTAT Crops and livestock products, assuming typical weights of animals from various sources, average 16% protein in whole animal and Jones factor 6.25 for nitrogen to protein (standard).

**How the numbers are derived**

- Numbers of imported live animals by type from FAOSTAT (trade in live animals), times a live weight per animal type (animal_weights sheet of N_parameters.xlsx), 16% protein (range 14–22%) and 1/6.25 for N in protein.

**Interpretation**

- The flow is negligible, below 0.05 kt N per year; Norway imports very few live animals.
