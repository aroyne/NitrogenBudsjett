### Flow Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
Is taken from trade data, SSB table 08801. Soybeans and soybean meal (HS 1201 and 1208.10) are not included. Soybeans are imported mainly for feed and crushed in Norway (Eidem & Ruud, 2022)<!--cite:eidem_for-_2022-->. The soy meal used in feed for farm animals is counted in RW.RW-AG.MM-Animal feed import-Nmix, and the soy used in fish feed is part of RW.RW-HY.AC-Aquaculture feed import-Nmix, where all plant-based ingredients are counted as imported. Imported soybeans amount to 18–25 ktN/year. Fish waste and by-products not fit for human consumption (HS 0511), which are processed in the food and feed industry, are included.
