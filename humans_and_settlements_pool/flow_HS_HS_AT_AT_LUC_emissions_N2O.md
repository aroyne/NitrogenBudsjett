# LUC Emissions (N2O)

### Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
#### Flow description

- **HS.HS-AT.AT-LUC emissions-N2O** is N2O from soils in settlements, as reported under land use, land-use change and forestry (category 4.E Settlements in the greenhouse gas inventory).

#### Data sources

- N2O from "4.E. Settlements" in the UNFCCC Common Reporting Tables (CRT), Table 4, of the national greenhouse gas inventory ([Miljødirektoratet, NID 2026](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/publikasjoner/2026/mars-2026/greenhouse-gas-emissions-1990-2024-national-inventory-document/)).
- The emissions are not measured but calculated in the inventory from activity data (N inputs, areas, losses of soil carbon) and emission factors, following the IPCC guidelines. In 2024 the category consists of three parts (CRT Tables 4(I)–4(III)):
- N inputs to soils in settlements (Table 4(I), 0.034 kt N2O): 1.9 kt N of organic fertilizers (such as sewage sludge and compost) applied to settlement areas, with direct emissions at an implied emission factor of 0.006 kg N2O–N per kg N, plus indirect emissions from the part of the N that volatilises (0.39 kt N) or is lost by leaching and run-off (0.44 kt N). Inorganic fertilizer used in settlements is reported under another category (notation key IE).
- Drained organic soils (Table 4(II), 0.072 kt N2O): area-based emission factors of about 1.5–1.6 kg N2O–N per hectare for forest and wetland converted to settlements (4.0 and 1.6 kha), and emissions from drained organic soils in settlements remaining settlements.
- Mineralisation of soil organic matter on land converted to settlements (Table 4(III), 0.051 kt N2O): the N released when soil carbon is lost (0.47 kt N on 73 kha in 2024, mainly forest and grassland converted to settlements) gives direct emissions and indirect emissions from leaching (0.011 kg N2O–N per kg N). Mineral soils in settlements remaining settlements are assumed to be in steady state and give no emissions.
- The inventory gives an uncertainty of 201% (95% interval).

#### Assumptions

- The uncertainty is modelled as a lognormal distribution with the reported value as median.

#### Interpretations and comparisons

- The flow rose from about 0.06 kt N in 1990 to 0.1 kt N around 2010 with the expansion of settlement areas, and has been about 0.1 kt N per year since.
- The 1.9 kt N of organic fertilizer on settlement areas in the inventory is of the same size as the sewage sludge and biologically treated waste delivered to green areas and soil producers in this model (PR.WW-HS.HS-Sewage sludge fertilizer-Nmix and PR.SO-HS.HS-Biologically treated organic waste-Nmix, together about 2.3 kt N in 2024).
