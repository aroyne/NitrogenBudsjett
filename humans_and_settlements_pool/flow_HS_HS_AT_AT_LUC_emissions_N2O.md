# LUC Emissions (N2O)

### Description
<!-- MANUAL:FLOW_DESCRIPTION:START -->
**HS.HS-AT.AT-LUC emissions-N2O** is taken from UNFCCC Common reporting tables, Table 4.

**How the numbers are derived**

- The flow is the N2O from "4.E. Settlements" in CRT Table 4 of the national greenhouse gas inventory ([Miljødirektoratet, NID 2026](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/publikasjoner/2026/mars-2026/greenhouse-gas-emissions-1990-2024-national-inventory-document/)), calculated with IPCC methods.
- In 2024 it consists of N2O from fertilizer applied on settlement areas (Table 4(I), 0.034 kt N2O; the inventory assumes 1.9 kt N fertilizer applied, close to the 2% of mineral fertilizer use assumed for MP.OP-HS.HS-Mineral fertilizer-Nmix), from mineralisation of soil organic matter when land is converted to settlements (Table 4(III), 0.051 kt N2O) and from drained organic soils under settlements (Table 4(II), 0.072 kt N2O).
- N2O is converted to N with the factor 28/44.
- The inventory gives an uncertainty of 201% (95% interval), modelled as a lognormal distribution with the reported value as median.

**Interpretation**

- The flow rose from about 0.06 kt N in 1990 to 0.1 kt N around 2010 with the expansion of settlement areas, and has been about 0.1 kt N per year since.
